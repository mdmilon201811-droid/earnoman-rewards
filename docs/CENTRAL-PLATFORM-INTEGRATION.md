# Central platform integration plan

This repository remains an independent product. The Central AI Command Center may later receive limited, authenticated operational summaries through secured APIs.

## Shared principles
- Keep product user data, credentials and financial ledgers separated.
- Use role-based access, least privilege and audit logs.
- Central monitoring may aggregate uptime, errors and usage summaries.
- Human approval is required for withdrawals, bank changes, high-value refunds, pricing or commission changes, administrator changes, ownership/legal declarations and destructive operations.
- Complete country-specific legal, privacy, advertising, tax and payment-provider reviews before launch.

## Not implemented
No live central API, shared authentication, payment integration, AI provider integration or remote command execution is connected by this documentation.

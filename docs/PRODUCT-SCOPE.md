# EarnWorld Rewards — product scope

## Goal
An international rewards platform with tasks, surveys, daily activities and referrals.

## Reward integrity
- Demo points have no cash value until a funded and legally reviewed rewards program is launched.
- Verify task and survey completion on the server before crediting points.
- Publish clear eligibility, point rules, conversion rates, withdrawal minimums, fees and processing times before enabling payouts.
- Support only countries and payment methods that have passed provider, KYC/AML, tax and legal checks.
- Never fund earlier users' rewards from later users' deposits.
- Do not use recruitment-only cash commissions. Any referral benefit must be transparent, lawful and funded by genuine platform revenue.

## Not implemented
Real surveys, fraud-resistant verification, funded rewards, cash conversion, withdrawals, payment integrations and production account security are not complete.
